Japan in Bloom

A celebration of cherry blossoms, hanami traditions, and the brief beauty of spring across Japan.

The meaning of sakura

Cherry blossoms are a symbol of renewal, beauty, and impermanence. Their short flowering period makes each spring feel rare and memorable.

Hanami traditions

Picnics beneath the trees

Families, friends, and coworkers gather under blooming branches to eat and enjoy the season.

Yozakura

At night, lanterns illuminate the blossoms and create a quieter evening atmosphere.

Seasonal food

Sakura-themed sweets, tea, and bento boxes appear throughout Japan during blossom season.

Following the bloom

The cherry blossom season begins earlier in southern Japan and gradually moves north as temperatures rise.
